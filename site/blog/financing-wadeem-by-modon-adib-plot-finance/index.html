Financing Wadeem Gardens by Modon with ADIB off-plan finance

Modon and Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB) have introduced Abu Dhabi's first off-plan home financing — and Wadeem Gardens on Hudayriyat Island is the flagship. Eligible buyers can now finance up to 75% of the villa on a 25/75 plan, instead of funding the whole purchase in cash during construction. Here's exactly how it works.

About Wadeem Gardens

Wadeem Gardens by Modon is a connected district on Hudayriyat Island, Abu Dhabi — bringing together homes, work, learning, wellness and community in one place. It offers luxury 4- to 6-bedroom villas, with handover scheduled for Q2 2031. Indicative starting prices:

  • 4-bedroom villa — from AED 8.7M (approx. 532 sqm)
  • 5-bedroom villa — from AED 10.2M (approx. 630 sqm)
  • 6-bedroom villa — from AED 11.6M (approx. 720 sqm)

Abu Dhabi's first off-plan home financing

Historically, buying off-plan in Abu Dhabi meant funding the entire purchase yourself during construction, with a mortgage only possible at handover. Modon and ADIB have changed that: their partnership lets eligible buyers finance up to 75% of the property with ADIB during the off-plan and construction phase — a first for the emirate. The financing is Sharia-compliant, through ADIB.

The 25/75 plan explained

On Wadeem Gardens the structure works out so you fund roughly 25% and ADIB finances about 75%:

  • 5% — down payment on booking / reservation
  • 15% — during construction, in 5% instalments (around months 8, 14 and 20)
  • 20% — financed by ADIB during the construction phase (reduces your out-of-pocket cost)
  • 5% — final buyer payment at completion / handover
  • 55% — ADIB mortgage at handover (Q2 2031)

So across the journey you pay about 25% (5% + 15% + 5%) and ADIB provides the remaining ~75% (20% during construction + 55% mortgage at handover) — subject to ADIB's eligibility, valuation and credit approval.

Worked example (illustrative). On a 4-bedroom villa from AED 8.7M, your own contribution across the 25/75 plan is roughly AED 2.18M, with ADIB financing around AED 6.52M — plus transaction costs (DLD, registration, valuation and fees). Your exact figures depend on the unit price and ADIB's assessment of your profile; we confirm them before you commit.

Who can apply

The financing is open to new and existing ADIB customers — you don't need to already bank with ADIB. Approval is subject to ADIB's standard eligibility checks, income criteria, valuation and credit policy. Getting your eligibility confirmed before you reserve is the smart move, so you know exactly what you can commit to.
