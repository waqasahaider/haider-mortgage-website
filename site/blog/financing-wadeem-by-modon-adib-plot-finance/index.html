Financing Wadeem by Modon: your ADIB plot finance options

Wadeem by Modon sold out in 72 hours — so if you've secured a plot on Hudayriyat Island (or you're eyeing a resale or the next release), the big question is how to finance it. Land plots are financed differently from ready homes, and ADIB's Islamic Plot Finance is one of the main routes. Here's how it works.

About Wadeem by Modon

Launched in July 2025 by Modon Properties, Wadeem is the first residential plot community on Hudayriyat Island, opposite Abu Dhabi's prestigious Al Bateen and around 20 minutes from Downtown. It offers more than 1,700 plots ranging from roughly 608 to 925 sqm — each large enough for a four- to six-bedroom villa with a pool — with a central hub, retail, a school, a mosque and landscaped parks. Prices started around AED 3.7 million, with handover targeted for December 2028. Demand was extraordinary: the release reportedly sold out within 72 hours.

Because Wadeem is sold as off-plan land rather than a finished home, financing it needs a specific approach — and a bit of planning for the villa you'll eventually build.

Why financing a plot is different

Most UAE mortgages are for completed or under-construction homes. A bare residential plot is a different product: banks that offer it typically finance a lower percentage of the land value and set their own criteria for which communities they'll lend on. On top of that, buying the land is only step one — you'll need a separate plan to finance building the villa. Getting both parts right from the start is where good advice pays for itself.

ADIB Plot Finance at a glance

Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB) offers a dedicated, Sharia-compliant Plot Finance product for buying residential land at freehold developments in Abu Dhabi and Dubai. Key features:

  • Up to 60% financing of the plot value — i.e. a 40% down payment
  • Islamic (Murabaha-based) structure with fixed profit rates
  • Salaried eligibility: from AED 10,000/month with salary transfer (AED 15,000 without)
  • Self-employed: from around AED 3,000,000 annual turnover
  • Age: 21–70 for UAE nationals, 21–65 for expats (at finance maturity)
  • Early-settlement waiver on up to 30% of the outstanding balance each year

ADIB Plot Finance can also cover plot handover payments, which matters for an off-plan release like Wadeem where you pay the developer in stages up to handover.

Worked example (illustrative). On a AED 3,700,000 Wadeem plot at 60% financing, you'd fund roughly AED 1,480,000 as the 40% down payment and finance about AED 2,220,000, plus transaction costs (DLD, mortgage registration, valuation and fees). Your actual figures depend on the bank, your profile and the final plot price — we confirm exact numbers before you commit.

Don't forget: building your villa

Plot finance covers the land. To build your four- to six-bedroom villa you'll need construction / self-build finance, which works on a staged basis against your build contract and drawdowns. Planning the land purchase and the build together — rather than sorting the plot first and worrying about the build later — keeps your total cost of finance under control and avoids nasty surprises at construction stage.
